02 / Challenge

25+ years
of policy logic.

Constant vendor churn.
Limited institutional memory.

Knowledge kept leaving

The codebase was maintained across rotating offshore vendor teams. Context often disappeared when engineers left.

The code became the specification

Documentation was limited, forcing teams to reconstruct intent from Java, DB2 behavior, stored procedures, tickets and previous fixes.

The client stayed reactive

Knowledge gaps made changes riskier, increased production issues, and slowed delivery.
